Method: match a fully vocalised Corpus lemma to Qutrub's published lexicon.
Form I additionally requires matching root radicals and a unique resulting
conjugation table across lexical candidates. Augmented verb lemmas must also
exist in the lexicon. Unknown and ambiguous entries are omitted. These are
active dictionary forms (he, he, you masculine singular), not an assertion
about the person, voice or tense in the selected ayah. Verbal nouns and
participles are not generated by this integration.
